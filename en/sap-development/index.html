Services

How we support your SAP landscape

ABAP and ABAP OO

New development, further development and optimisation of customer owned applications. Migration of grown developments to Clean ABAP, including quality assurance with ATC.

Enhancements to the standard

Adapting SAP standard processes through BAdIs, user exits, enhancement spots and class inheritance, without bending the standard further than necessary.

Interfaces and integration

OData and SOAP web services, IDoc processing, ALE and RFC, connection of third party systems and geographic information systems, and integration in the BTP environment.

SAP Business Workflow

Design and development of approval and processing flows, event driven start, distribution of worklists, deadlines and resubmissions.

S/4HANA and HANA

Support for transformation projects in greenfield and brownfield, code adaptation and simplification items, CDS views, code pushdown and performance analysis.

Fiori, UI5 and RAP

Development and extension of Fiori applications, ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model, and replacement of outdated user interfaces with modern apps.

Sourcing and materials management

Functional consulting in S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement and SAP MM, from release strategies and output management to vendor evaluation and source lists.

Operations and error analysis

Support in ongoing line operation, debugging and root cause analysis, incident handling, technical documentation and transport control.

Experience

Industries and environments

Automotive, finance, agricultural engineering, insurance and the public sector. In the public sector in particular, what counts alongside development is the ability to work into grown specialist procedures and to deliver dependably during live operation.
