What helps us in the first step

  • Which process is this about, and who works with it today?
  • What is the process handled with at the moment, for example Excel, Access or paper?
  • How many people should be able to work with it at the same time in future?
  • Are there systems it has to connect to, an ERP for instance?
  • Is there a date by which the solution has to be in place?

If you cannot answer all of these, that is no obstacle. These are exactly the questions we work through together in the first conversation.

What happens next

  1. First conversation

    Around thirty minutes, by video or phone. Afterwards both sides know whether it fits.

  2. Written proposal

    You receive a proposal with work packages and the estimated effort per package, together with the terms of billing, the time frame and the rights to the result.

  3. Start

    After your approval we begin with the analysis. You see interim results early and regularly, not only at the end.
